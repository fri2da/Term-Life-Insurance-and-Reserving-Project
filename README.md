# Term-Life-Insurance-and-Reserving-Project
Term Life Insurance Pricing and Reserving Project

This project explores the application of actuarial principles in term life insurance pricing and reserving. Using Excel, I developed an actuarial pricing framework that applies mortality assumptions, life table functions and the equivalence principle to estimate appropriate insurance premiums.

The project involves cleaning and analysing policyholder data, constructing a mortality life table, calculating commutation functions, and determining net and loaded annual premiums based on policyholders' ages and sums assured. It also incorporates portfolio analysis, risk classification and sensitivity testing to examine how changes in interest rate assumptions influence premium income.

Through this project, I applied actuarial mathematics and Excel functions to practical life insurance problems while strengthening my understanding of mortality modelling, premium determination, financial risk assessment and the role of assumptions in actuarial decision-making.

Key areas covered:

- Mortality table construction and analysis.
- Commutation functions and actuarial pricing factors.
- Term life insurance premium calculations using the equivalence principle.
- Policyholder data cleaning and risk classification.
- Portfolio analysis and dashboard development.
- Interest rate sensitivity analysis.

Tools: Microsoft Excel

Learning objective: To develop practical actuarial skills in life insurance pricing, mortality analysis and financial risk assessment.
